# Working line

`main` is protected. Do not commit to it, and do not merge work into it.

`master` is the integration branch. How a change is accepted, and how roadmap issues stay current, is [the development process](development-process.md).

Behavior on this line is the code on `master` and the accepted ADRs. Unmerged preparation branches are history. They do not authorize work, and they do not block it.
